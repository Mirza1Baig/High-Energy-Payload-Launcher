Drive link for Payload Launcher(catapult) Attached above.
Took inspiration for shape of throwing arm from the suspension wishbones of a car.
Used Polyethylene sheets as padding at the contact surface upon impact to reduce the sudden impulse and provide coushioning.
Hooks and joints are of high-strength structural steel and general body is Aluminum 6061 T6.
The base plate was fully constrained to isolate the structural stress analysis of the primary throwing arm and tower, 
under the assumption of continuous, rigid anchor fixing the base to the ground.
Mechanism includes:
1. Spring pulling the throwing arm while loading.
2. Hook attached to throwing arm which keeps the arm at rest after loading
3. Lever attached to the hook, on pulling lever, force due to spring rotates the arm.
4. Arm is stopped by a tower at around 90 degrees which launches the 10kg payload at 10m/s speed.
5. Stopped is a big structure reinforced by triangular beams to distribute force and reduce bulkiness.
